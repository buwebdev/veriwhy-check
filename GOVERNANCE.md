# Governance

Author and project maintainer: Richard Krasso

VeriWhy Check is currently maintained under a benevolent-maintainer model.
Richard Krasso sets the educational direction, approves releases, publishes
assessment profiles, and makes final decisions about security, privacy, and
course alignment.

Community members may propose changes through issues and pull requests.
Decisions prioritize functional accuracy, actionable student feedback,
beginner accessibility, reproducibility, local privacy, and sustainable course
maintenance. A public check does not become an official grading policy merely
because it is included in this repository.
